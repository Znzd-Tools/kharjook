# E-13 Expense plan

## Goal

The user can plan one Jalali month by hand and accept suggestions from installments, checks, recurring expenses, and subscriptions.

## In scope

- Manual lines
- Suggestions
- Month total

## Out of scope

- Turning a plan line into a posted transaction by itself
