# E-04 Wallets and savings pots

## Goal

The user can keep cash accounts in IRT, USD, TRY, and EUR, store payment details, and track savings pots inside a wallet.

## In scope

- Wallet lifecycle
- Payment details
- Savings pots
- Balance from opening balance plus transactions

## Out of scope

- Bank sync
