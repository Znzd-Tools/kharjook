# E-17 Budget envelopes

## Goal

The user gives every toman of a month's income a job: one envelope per expense category, money moved between envelopes, leftovers carried to next month.

## In scope

- Envelope per expense category (monthly amount = the category cap)
- One-month override and moves between envelopes
- Rollover of leftover or overspend
- Month summary: income, assigned, spent, unassigned

## Out of scope

- Envelopes for income or asset categories
