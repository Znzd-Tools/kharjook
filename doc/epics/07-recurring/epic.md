# E-07 Recurring transactions

## Goal

Income and expense templates post themselves on a Jalali schedule, including catch-up, without duplicates.

## In scope

- Template CRUD
- Scheduled generation and idempotent runs

## Out of scope

- Recurring buys, sells, or transfers
