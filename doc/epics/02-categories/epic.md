# E-02 Categories and spending caps

## Goal

The user can keep three category trees and put a monthly toman cap on an expense category.

## In scope

- Asset, income, and expense trees
- Reorder
- Delete that uncategorises dependents
- Monthly spending cap

## Out of scope

- Sharing categories across users
